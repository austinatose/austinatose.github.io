Personal Google Workspace integration

Austin's MCP

Austin's MCP connects Austin's own Google account to his privately operated Hermes assistants, including Jeb. It supports personal tasks involving Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Gmail, and Calendar, according to the permissions granted and the tools enabled.

This is a personal-use integration, not a public sign-up service. The website provides information only; it does not sign visitors in or access their Google accounts.

How it works

Google OAuth lets Austin grant and revoke access without giving the integration his Google password. The assistant uses the approved permissions to retrieve information and perform requested tasks. Relevant information may be sent to the configured AI model provider to process those tasks.

These public pages contain no Google account data, OAuth credentials, or assistant conversations.
