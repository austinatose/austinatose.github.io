Privacy policy

This policy describes Austin's privately operated Google Workspace integration for his Hermes assistants, including Jeb. It is intended for Austin's own account and personal use, not as a service offered to the public.

Google information accessed

Depending on the OAuth scopes granted, the tools enabled, and the task, the integration may access Google Drive file metadata and contents; Google Docs and Sheets content; Gmail messages, labels, and drafts; and Google Calendar lists and events. Some granted permissions also allow creating or modifying content. The Google consent screen lists the permissions requested.

Purpose and processing

Access is used for personal assistance, such as finding information, summarising documents, checking schedules, and performing tasks Austin requests or configures. Relevant task content may be transmitted to the AI model provider configured in Hermes and to services explicitly used to carry out the task. The integration is not used to sell Google user data or serve advertising.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in private configuration on the devices running the integration, not on this public website. Retrieved information, tool results, conversation history, and generated files may be retained in private assistant storage and backups until Austin deletes them or the relevant retention settings remove them. Providers involved in processing apply their own retention policies and account settings.

Sharing and access

The integration is personally operated by Austin. Google data is not published by this website. Task-related data may be processed by the configured model or tool providers as described above. This integration is not a public multi-user service.

Revoking access and deleting data

Austin can revoke the integration's access through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future access through that grant; it does not automatically delete copies already retained in assistant history, files, provider systems, or backups. Local tokens and retained assistant data can be deleted from the systems that hold them; provider-held data is subject to the provider's controls and policies.

This website

These informational pages have no sign-in form, analytics script, or tracking cookie added by this project. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process routine request and connection information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Questions and deletion requests: austinliuzirui@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when the integration changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.
